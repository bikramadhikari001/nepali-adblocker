About

Nepali Adblocker v1.0.0

A lightweight Chrome extension that blocks ads on Nepali websites, inspired by uBlock Origin.

Blocks ads on the top 100 Nepali websites with special focus on news sites like OnlineKhabar, Setopati, eKantipur, and more.

Built with ❤️ for Nepali internet users.
